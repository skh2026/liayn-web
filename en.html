Since April 2023 LIAYN: K-Volunteer Group Driven by AI Humanism

While AI tools have seamlessly integrated into the daily lives of Gen Z and Millennials, the story is very different for South Korea’s older generations—the very people who built the nation's industrial foundation. For those aged 65 and older, the rapid pace of tech adoption creates deep digital isolation and widens the social gap between generations.

Bridging this gap requires more than just teaching seniors how to use tech. It calls for a fundamentally human-centered approach to technology. LIAYN leverages generative AI to restore our elders' most cherished youth memories—practicing "AI Humanism" to uphold human dignity, inspire self-confidence, and rebuild meaningful social connections across generations.

PROJECTOur 3-Step Action Model

01. Memory Restoration Content

We leverage generative AI to bring our elders' past youth back to life alongside their present selves. These story posts and short-form videos are published at least four times a month across our digital channels.

02. Spontaneous Social Engagement

Through targeted hashtag campaigns, we invite younger generations to connect with these stories, driving genuine engagement with a goal of over 100 supportive comments and interactions monthly.

03. Offline Emotional Delivery

We collect warm online messages left by younger users, print them, and deliver them directly to our elders in person (targeting 20+ recipients)—turning online empathy into real-world comfort and intergenerational conversation.
